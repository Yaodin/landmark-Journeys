# Rail journeys: 25 atlas candidates

Ranked for historical recognition, geographic variety, and route recoverability. `High` means a specific historic rail alignment can probably be traced from maps or railway GIS; `Medium` means the documented stops are sound but the historical alignment or service pattern needs further archival work. These are research candidates, not finished route geometries.

**Mapping rule:** Preserve a separate `historical_alignment` and `service_date`. A modern train sharing a name with a historical service should not silently inherit its route. Use separate features for rail and ferry/ship segments on the 1883 Express d'Orient.
