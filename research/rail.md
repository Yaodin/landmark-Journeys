# Rail journeys: 25 atlas candidates

Ranked for historical recognition, geographic variety, and route recoverability. `High` means a specific historic rail alignment can probably be traced from maps or railway GIS; `Medium` means the documented stops are sound but the historical alignment or service pattern needs further archival work. All 25 currently have published atlas geometry, but generated samples between anchors are illustrative and are **not** surveyed track centerlines.

**Mapping rule:** Preserve a separate `historical_alignment` and `service_date`. A modern train sharing a name with a historical service should not silently inherit its route. Use separate features for rail and ferry/ship segments on the 1883 Express d'Orient.

## Alignment pass, 2026-09-24

This pass retained all 25 records and IDs. It improved three source-backed descriptions/alignments and left unsupported spans schematic:

| Route | Evidence and change | Still unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Golden Spike, 1869 | [NPS locomotive history](https://www.nps.gov/gosp/learn/historyculture/everlasting-steam-the-story-of-jupiter-and-no-119.htm) makes clear that the Omaha and Sacramento lines are **network-construction approaches**, not Jupiter and No. 119's complete May 10 trips. [NPS has a digitized park-local 1869 grade](https://irmadev.nps.gov/DataStore/Reference/Profile/1020405), but the file is not available from that catalog page. | Trace the actual laid rail near Promontory, excluding parallel unfinished grades, from a licensed GIS extract or georeferenced historic survey. Long-distance town-to-town lines remain corridors. |
| California Zephyr, 1949 | The [railroad-issued December 1949 timetable](https://streamlinermemories.info/RG/WP49-12TT.pdf) supports the Oakland–Niles–Pleasanton–Stockton–Sacramento approach, Feather River line and Burlington route through southern Nebraska/Iowa. Added geographic anchors for those confirmed corridors and removed the direct Oakland–Sacramento chord. The [inaugural-date museum image](https://www.californiazephyr.org/exhibits/timetables/) is only a cover, not a route schedule. | The December schedule is a nine-month-later proxy for March 1949; continuity is inferred. Timetable places are not measured track vertices or necessarily inaugural stops. Detailed Feather River, Moffat and Burlington curvature still needs period-alignment tracing. |
| Eurostar, 1994 | The [UK Department for Transport's HS1 evaluation](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/682f37fbb33f68eaba9539cd/hs1-second-evaluation.pdf) documents the Waterloo classic-network route via Nine Elms, the Brixton/Herne Hill side and Orpington–Sevenoaks–Tonbridge. Removed the erroneous Clapham Junction excursion and added pre-HS1 junction waypoints. | The evidence reconstructs the typical pre-HS1 route, not the inaugural train's minute-by-minute path. Tunnel portals and LGV Nord geometry remain approximate. |
| Stockton–Darlington, 1825 | [Historic England's listed earthworks](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1484556) establish that the original Preston Park grade lies about 250 m east of its 1852 replacement. The existing Eaglescliffe place anchor is explicitly labeled a geographic cue, not an opening-day station. | Full [archaeological route mapping](https://historicengland.org.uk/research/results/reports/28-2019) should be converted into a licensed centerline before replacing the schematic line. |
| Suffrage Special, 1916 | The [original route map](https://www.loc.gov/item/mnwp000270/) and [Library of Congress chronology](https://www.loc.gov/static/collections/women-of-protest/images/detchron.pdf) confirm the cross-country tour, but not exact rails; Kansas and Colorado fragments remain separate. | Transcribe the 14-page 1916 itinerary and match each leg to contemporary railroad timetables before joining fragments. |

Other priority alignments remain: the 1883 *Express d'Orient* ferry and ship legs, the 1916 Circum-Baikal and Amur track, original Rogers Pass loops, 1912 Florida Keys bridges, 1930 Furka summit grade, 1955 Canadian Pacific branches, 1970 Indian Pacific junctions, and 1910 Bernina loops. Six formerly `source-derived` rail segment labels were conservatively changed to `historical-corridor`: their stations and lines are historically documented, but no measured centerline has been imported. Their high anchor-confidence labels remain distinct from route-shape fidelity.
